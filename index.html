How it works

From first idea to final moment

  1. Tell us your plan. Share your event type, date, location, guest count and budget range.
  2. Receive a tailored proposal. We outline the recommended scope, suppliers, timeline and costs.
  3. Confirm the details. Once scope, vendor availability, written terms and advance payment are agreed, the booking is confirmed.
  4. Enjoy the occasion. Our team coordinates the agreed arrangements and event-day checklist.
